Artwork provenance
The app's catalogue comes from arkpedia/arkpedia-data; its source revision
is recorded in src/catalogue-source.json. Enemy model sources and hashes are
recorded separately in src/enemy-catalogue-source.json. Models and furniture are fetched
from pinned revisions of arkpedia/arkpedia-sd-assets, not bundled artwork.
The asset repository records sources and hashes in its manifests and NOTICE.md:
https://github.com/arkpedia/arkpedia-sd-assets
Its MIT license covers its original scripts only, not the game assets.

Thanks to the community asset maintainers, including fexli/ArknightsResource,
